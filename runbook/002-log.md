# H-02 — THE NERVOUS SYSTEM

Phase log. Three lines per session: what I did, what broke, what I'd do differently.

## 2026-09-03 · ran from the evening of the 2nd
- **Did:** Settled the topic tree on paper first (`config/mosquitto/TOPICS.md`) and
  found three gaps in the masterplan sketch: no liveness convention, no retained-vs-
  event rule, and `magi/light/cmd` as "the only write topic" — which won't survive
  Home Assistant. Replaced with `status` (retained + LWT) on every publisher, state
  retained / events not, and `…/set` as the only writable suffix, which is what makes
  the ACL one line instead of twelve. Mosquitto up with auth and ACLs from the first
  boot, on its own `bus` network rather than `edge` — Caddy doesn't proxy MQTT and
  separate buses keep Suricata's eventual scope honest. First publisher (`magi-sys.py`)
  reports `magi/sys/*` and `magi/svc/*` over one long-lived connection, because a
  `mosquitto_pub` on a timer disconnects cleanly every 15 s and can never have a
  meaningful will. Verified rather than assumed: anonymous refused, ACL denying a
  cross-root write, retained state surviving a broker restart off disk, the will firing
  on SIGKILL but not on `systemctl stop`, and a reboot with the port bound to a tailnet
  address that didn't exist when Docker asked for it.
- **Broke:** Mosquitto 2.1 warns — and says future versions will refuse — on an
  `acl_file` that's world-readable or not owned by the broker, and `git pull` resets a
  tracked config to 0644 every time it changes; `50-mqtt.sh` now fixes the mode on
  deploy. That script also exists because `docker compose up -d` compares the service
  definition, not the contents of bind-mounted files, so a changed `.conf` sits on disk
  unread and everything looks deployed. `55-sys-publisher.sh` shipped with two bugs: a
  `test -f` against a file inside 0700-root `/etc/magi` reports "missing" and re-prompts
  for the password every run, and `install /dev/stdin` fails under sudo when stdin is a
  pipe. And I told Akhil to look for a `Denied PUBLISH` line in the broker log — there
  isn't one. Mosquitto doesn't log ACL denials at `notice`/`information`, so a refused
  publish is silent from **both** ends: no error to the publisher, no line in the log.
- **Differently:** Read what mosquitto logs and at which level *before* saying what to
  look for. Naming a log line that doesn't exist is worse than saying nothing — the
  absence reads as "the ACL isn't loaded", which is the opposite of the truth. Same
  root cause as the file-permission trap: both were in mosquitto's own documented
  behaviour, and both were found by running it rather than by reading first.
